# Subspace.money Product Teardown

## Product Intern Assignment Submission

This repository contains my product teardown and UX analysis of **Subspace.money**, created as part of the Product Intern Assignment.

The analysis was conducted through real platform exploration, marketplace interaction, navigation testing, and UX evaluation across multiple product flows.

---

# Objective

The goal of this teardown was to identify high-impact product, UX, and marketplace improvement opportunities that can enhance:

- User onboarding clarity
- Marketplace trust
- Product discovery
- Navigation efficiency
- Personalization
- Long-term user engagement

---

# Areas Explored

- Homepage UX & positioning
- Shared subscription marketplace
- Trust signals & credibility
- Discovery experience
- Category navigation
- Product hierarchy
- Recommendation opportunities
- Strategic prioritization

---

# Key Feedback Areas

## 1. Homepage Positioning & UX Clarity
- Promotional density creates cognitive overload
- Core value proposition is not immediately clear
- Better onboarding hierarchy recommended

## 2. Shared Subscription Experience & Trust Signals
- Strong pricing visibility and inventory depth
- Missing verified groups, ratings, and trust indicators
- Opportunity to improve purchase confidence

## 3. Discovery Experience & Category Navigation
- Category overload impacts discoverability
- Inconsistent product card hierarchy
- Personalization and recommendation systems underutilized

---

# Strategic Recommendations

- Improve onboarding clarity
- Add trust-focused marketplace signals
- Simplify discovery navigation
- Introduce personalized recommendations
- Standardize card and content hierarchy
